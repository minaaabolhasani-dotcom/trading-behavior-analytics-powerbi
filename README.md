# Trading Behavior & Risk Analytics | Power BI

An independent, exploratory financial analytics project using Power BI to examine trading performance, risk exposure, and behavioral patterns in historical XAUUSD *demo* trading data.

Trade-level records are transformed into interactive dashboards to explore associations between trading decisions, risk management, and financial outcomes.

## Key Findings

Descriptive observations from the analyzed sample, not causal conclusions.

- *Rule Compliance: Trades classified as rule-compliant showed better performance than rule-violating trades.
- Post-Loss Behavior: Following losing trades, longer intervals between trades and higher risk exposure and position sizes were observed.
- Session Analysis: Trading frequency and performance varied across market sessions, particularly during the London–New York overlap.

## Analytical Scope

- Performance & Risk: Profitability, win rate, R-based performance, risk distribution, and position sizing.
- Behavioral Analysis: FOMO, revenge trading, overconfidence, and rule compliance.
- Sequential Behavior: Trading decisions following winning and losing trades.
- Market & News Analysis: Trading activity and outcomes across market sessions, entry hours, and economic news windows.

## Tools & Methodology

Power BI | Power Query | DAX | Data Modeling | Financial & Risk Analytics | Data Visualization

Historical trade records were prepared and modeled to develop interactive dashboards and analytical measures.

Behavioral classifications were informed by trading observations and retrospective reviews of recorded sessions.

## Limitations

- Based on personal XAUUSD demo trading records.
- Behavioral labels may involve subjective judgment and retrospective bias.
- Findings are sample-specific and may not generalize to other traders or market conditions.
- Observed associations do not establish causality or demonstrate a validated trading strategy.
